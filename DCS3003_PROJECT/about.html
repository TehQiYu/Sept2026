About Aspec

Gear made by people who live at their desk.

Our story

Aspec started in Johor Bahru with a simple frustration: good computer accessories were either overpriced or disappointing. We wanted keyboards that felt great, mice that disappeared in your hand and cables that didn't fail after six months.

So we began designing our own. Every Aspec product is tested by our team for weeks before it ever reaches a customer, and we only sell things we'd happily use ourselves.

Today we ship to thousands of gamers, designers, developers and students, and we're only getting started.
